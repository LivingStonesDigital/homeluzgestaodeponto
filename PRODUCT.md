# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Employees clocking in and out daily, often on mobile in a hurry. HR admins reviewing approvals, corrections, and reports on desktop. The employee wants zero friction; the admin wants clarity and confidence.

## Product Purpose

Electronic time clock system that makes registering work hours simple, reliable, and transparent. Success means employees can clock in and out in seconds, and admins can review and approve with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Professional and trustworthy, yet warm and approachable. Inspired by Brazilian fintech (Nubank, PicPay) — clean, modern, human. Voice is direct, clear, and respectful. No jargon, no fluff.

## Anti-references

Not playful, gamified, or cartoonish. No bouncing illustrations, no achievement badges, no "fun" detours. This is a serious tool that happens to be pleasant to use. Also not the cold, dense corporate aesthetic of legacy SAP/Oracle HR portals.

## Design Principles

1. **Confiança em cada batida** — every interaction must feel reliable, accurate, and transparent. The system earns trust by being predictable and honest.

2. **Simplicidade radical** — clocking in and out is a single tap. No multi-step flows, no confirmation dialogs for the primary action. Friction is the enemy.

3. **Mobile-first, desktop-capable** — the primary touchpoint is a phone in a pocket. Every screen works great at 375px and scales up gracefully for admin desktop workflows.

4. **Calm technology** — the tool fades into the background. It should not demand attention, make noise, or create anxiety. It simply works.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Support for reduced motion (already implemented via view transitions). Adequate touch targets for mobile use.
